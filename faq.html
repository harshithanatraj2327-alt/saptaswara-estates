Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful answers to make your property journey feel clearer.

How do I begin searching for a property?

Start with the search form on the homepage. Add a location, choose buy or rent, select a property type and set a maximum budget. The current demo filters sample listings only.

Are the properties and prices on this website real?

No. The included names, prices, locations and property details are sample demonstration content, not live listings. Replace them with accurate, verified information before publishing.

Can you help me buy or rent?

The website is designed to present buying, renting, selling and consultation services. Update the service descriptions to reflect what your actual business offers.

Is support really available 24/7?

The design highlights 24/7 support as requested. Only advertise this promise if your team or support channel can genuinely provide it. Add the correct phone number, email or messaging link.

How do I arrange a viewing?

Use the contact page to express interest. In this demo, the contact form displays a confirmation message but does not deliver submissions; connect it to an email or CRM service to receive enquiries.

How do you keep property searches safe?

Before making payments or signing documents, independently verify ownership, title documents, approvals, terms and the identity of the parties involved. A website badge alone is not proof of verification.

Can I request a tailored shortlist?

Yes—use the contact form to describe your preferred area, budget, property type, timing and must-have features. Connect the form to your business workflow to receive the request.
